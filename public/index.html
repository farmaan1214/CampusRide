About CampusRide

🎯 The Problem

College students struggle daily — spending too much on petrol, waiting endlessly for crowded public buses, and arriving late to classes. The stress of commuting alone eats into their time, money, and energy.

💡 Our Solution

Students travelling the same route to the same college can team up! A Rider (who owns a bike/scooter) offers their extra seat, and a Passenger finds that ride through CampusRide. Two people, one bike, shared costs — simple.

⚡ How It Works

Riders post their route to college. Passengers search for matching rides. Our system matches routes, calculates the fair fare at ₹5/km (split between both), and connects students instantly. Save fuel, save money, never be late again.

🌍 Our Mission

To make every college commute affordable, social, and sustainable — one shared ride at a time. Built by students, for students.
